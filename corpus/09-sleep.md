# Idle behavior

LumaDock Home enters sleep mode after 15 minutes without a connected computer by default.
Change the sleep delay in Settings, Device, Sleep timer in LumaDesk.
Available sleep delays are 5, 15, and 30 minutes.
The status light slowly pulses white while the dock is asleep.
Reconnect the HOST cable to wake a sleeping dock.
