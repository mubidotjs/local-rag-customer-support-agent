# Caring for the dock

Unplug power and all cables before cleaning LumaDock Home.
Clean the aluminum enclosure with a dry, lint-free cloth.
Do not spray liquid into ports or immerse the device in water.
Store the device in a dry place between 5 and 35 degrees Celsius.
Leave at least 3 centimeters of clearance around the ventilation slots during use.
