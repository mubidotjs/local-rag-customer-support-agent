LumaDock Home power guidance

LumaDock Home supplies up to 45 W of charging power to the connected laptop.
Only the supplied 65 W power adapter is approved for LumaDock Home.
The front USB-A port supplies up to 7.5 W to accessories.
An amber status light indicates that the power adapter is disconnected or insufficient.
Disconnect accessory devices and reconnect the supplied adapter if the light remains amber.
