# Delivery estimates

Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days after an order leaves the warehouse.
Express shipping takes 2 business days after dispatch.
Orders placed before 14:00 warehouse time on a business day are dispatched the same day.
A tracking number appears in the Orders tab after dispatch.
Address changes are available only before dispatch through the Orders tab.
LumaDock currently ships only within the fictional Northstar region.
