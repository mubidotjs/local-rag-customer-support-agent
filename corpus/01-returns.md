# Returns for LumaDock Home

LumaDock is a fictional desk hub used only for this demonstration.
Customers may return a LumaDock Home within 30 calendar days of delivery.
To start a return, open the Returns tab in the LumaDock desktop app and enter the order number.
The device, power adapter, and USB-C cable must be included in the return package.
Opened packaging is accepted if the device is undamaged.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days after inspection.
Shipping charges are not refundable unless the delivered device was defective.
